About

I am a software engineer who builds AI agents in production, and an MSc student in Human-Centred AI at the University of Groningen. My focus is human–AI interaction and autonomous agents.

Seeing AI change my own work made me want to help shape where it goes. I am moving my career into AI safety and governance, especially how people can keep meaningful control as AI systems become more capable.

Research interests

  • AI agents and human–agent collaboration. Where humans are still needed, and how people and agents should divide the work.
  • Accountability and governance of AI agents. When agents decide things, who understands each decision and who answers for it.
  • AI adoption in software teams. How to measure and govern AI-assisted development without losing quality or skills.
  • AI, work and education. The shrinking entry level, and the lag between what universities teach and what the AI job market needs.
